## Unit Convention

Measurements from psychrometric test facilities should be recorded as
physical quantities.  This means the measurements should take the form:

    Physical Quantity = [Magnitude] +/- [Uncertainty] [Unit]

In this definition then, a proper unit must be identified for each measurement
in order to give the magnitude context. This standard will not require a
specific unit convention, only a requirement the units of the recorded values.
However, the [SI unit system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SI) is strongly
recommended and preferred by the standard developers.

[SI prefixes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_prefix) to properly scale
quantities and make the data more readable.
